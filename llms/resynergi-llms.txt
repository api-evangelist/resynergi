# Resynergi

> Resynergi is an advanced plastic recycling technology company. It builds modular
> Continuous Microwave Assisted Pyrolysis (CMAP) systems — marketed as AMP Modules — that
> convert hard-to-recycle waste plastics (HDPE, LDPE, polypropylene, polystyrene) into
> circular pyrolysis oil (PyOil) and other petrochemical feedstocks. Founded in 2015 by
> Jason Tanne and Brian Bauer around microwave pyrolysis research from the University of
> Minnesota, and commercialized with Lummus Technology.

Important for agents: **Resynergi publishes no API.** There is no developer portal, no

## Product facts (from resynergi.com/our-solution/)

- ~5 tons of plastic waste processed per module per day
- ~1,000 gallons of PyOil produced per module per day
- 1+ kiloton of PyOil per module per year
- 440 sq ft floor space per module
- Two commercial motions: buy AMP Modules from Lummus Technology, or partner with Resynergi
  to operate them. Both route to a contact form; no prices are published.
